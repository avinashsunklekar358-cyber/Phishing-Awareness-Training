01 / UNDERSTAND THE THREAT

What is phishing?

Phishing is a scam where an attacker pretends to be a trusted person or organization to trick you into sharing information, sending money, or installing malicious software.

✉

Email phishing

Fake emails may impersonate banks, delivery companies, schools, or workplace support teams.

Check the sender
🌐

Fake websites

Look-alike login pages can steal credentials. A padlock or HTTPS alone does not prove a site is legitimate.

Check the domain
🧠

Social engineering

Attackers exploit fear, urgency, curiosity, authority, and trust to pressure you into acting.

Slow down
COMMON ATTACKER TACTICS
⏱ Urgency

“Act in 10 minutes or lose access.”

👔 Authority

“I'm from IT. Send your verification code.”

🎁 Reward

“You won a prize. Claim it now.”

😨 Fear

“Your account will be suspended.”

Can you spot the red flags?

These fictional examples represent common phishing patterns. They are not real messages or active links.

SUSPICIOUS EMAILExample 01

From: Account Team <support@bank-verify.example>

Subject: URGENT: Your account is locked!

Dear customer, your account will be permanently disabled in 30 minutes. Click below and enter your password to restore access.

RESTORE ACCOUNT
🚩 Warning signs

Unverified sender domain · Threatening deadline · Password request · Generic greeting

💡 Real-world pattern: Attackers commonly impersonate delivery services, financial institutions, and IT help desks. Instead of following a message link, open the official app or type the known website address yourself.

05 / IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Clicked a suspicious link? Act quickly.

01

Stop interacting

Close the suspicious page. Don't download files or enter more information.

02

Secure your accounts

If you entered a password, change it through the official site or app and enable MFA.

03

Report the incident

Notify your organization's IT/security team or the service provider through verified channels.

04

Check for further harm

If you downloaded a file, run an up-to-date security scan. If money or banking details were exposed, contact your bank immediately.

🔎 For additional guidance, visit CISA's phishing guidance. In India, you can report cybercrime through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
